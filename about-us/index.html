About Us

Who we are!

A club for builders, tinkerers & future founders.

WiE at the University of Waterloo brings engineering students together to connect, learn, and grow. We are dedicated to supporting women in engineering, celebrating diverse experiences, and inspiring the next generation of engineers.

Since 1992, our committee has worked to build a supportive community where students can explore opportunities, develop their skills, and form meaningful connections.

Engineering is for everyone. WiE welcomes people of all genders, orientations, and backgrounds to get involved, find their community, and help shape the future of engineering.

Our Mission!

The University of Waterloo acknowledges that much of our work takes place on the traditional territory of the Neutral, Anishinaabeg, and Haudenosaunee peoples. Our main campus is situated on the Haldimand Tract, the land granted to the Six Nations that includes six miles on each side of the Grand River. Our active work toward reconciliation takes place across our campuses through research, learning, teaching, and community building, and is co-ordinated within the Office of Indigenous Relations.
